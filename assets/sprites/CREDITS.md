## Removing these sprites

- Back to a placeholder for one class: remove its entry from `DIRECTIONAL` (and its imports).
- Back to the drawn shape for one monster: remove its entry from `ENEMY_SPRITES`.
- Switch off: set `SPRITES_ENABLED = false` in `src/render/sprites.ts`. Heroes go back to coloured circles,
  monsters to their drawn shapes.
- Remove completely: delete `src/render/sprites.ts` and this folder (`assets/sprites/`), then remove the
  `drawHeroSprite` / `drawEnemySprite` import, the `if (drawHeroSprite(...))` block and the
  `drawEnemySprite(...)` check in `drawEnemy`, all in `src/render/world.ts`.
